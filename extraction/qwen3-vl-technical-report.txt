Abstract
We introduce Qwen3-VL, the most capable visionlanguage model in the Qwen series to
date, achieving superior performance acros a broad range of multimodal benchmarks.
It natively suports interleaved contexts of up to 256K tokens, seamlesly integrat-
ing text, images, and video. The model family includes both dense (2B/4B/8B/32B)
and mixture-of-experts (30B-A3B/235B-A2B) variants to acomodate diverse la-
tencyquality trade-ofs. Qwen3-VL delivers thre core pilars: (i) markedly stronger
pure-text understanding, surpasing comparable text-only backbones in several cases;
(i) robust long-context comprehension with a native 256K-token window for both text
and interleaved multimodal inputs, enabling faithful retention, retrieval, and cros-
referencing acros long documents and videos; and (i) advanced multimodal reasoning
acros single-image, multi-image, and video tasks, demonstrating leading performance
on comprehensive evaluations such as MU and visual-math benchmarks (e.g., Math-
Vista and MathVision). Architecturaly, we introduce thre key upgrades: (i) an enhanced
interleaved-MRoPE for stronger spatialtemporal modeling acros images and video; (i)
DepStack integration, which efectively leverages multi-level ViT features to tighten
visionlanguage alignment; and (i) text-based time alignment for video, evolving from
T-RoPE to explicit textual timestamp alignment for more precise temporal grounding. To
balance text-only and multimodal learning objectives, we aply square-rot reweight-
ing, which bosts multimodal performance without compromising text capabilities.
We extend pretraining to a context length of 256K tokens and bifurcate post-training
into non-thinking and thinking variants to adres distinct aplication requirements.
Furthermore, we alocate aditional compute resources to the post-training phase to
further enhance model performance. Under comparable token budgets and latency
constraints, Qwen3-VL achieves superior performance in both dense and Mixture-of-
Experts (MoE) architectures. We envision Qwen3-VL serving as a foundational engine for
image-grounded reasoning, agentic decision-making, and multimodal code inteligence
in real-world workows.
