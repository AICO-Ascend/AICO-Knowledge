Abstract
Recent advancements in LM-based agents have demonstrated remarkable capa-
bilities in handling complex, knowledge-intensive tasks by integrating external
tols. Among diverse choices of tols, search tols play a pivotal role in acesing
vast external knowledge. However, open-source agents stil fal short of achieving
expert-level Search Inteligence, the ability to resolve ambiguous queries, generate
precise searches, analyze results, and conduct thorough exploration. Existing ap-
proaches fal short in scalability, efciency, and data quality. For example, smal

learning. This paper introduces ASearcher, an open-source project for large-scale
RL training of search agents. Our key contributions include: (1) Scalable fuly
asynchronous RL training that enables long-horizon search while maintaining
high training efciency. (2) A prompt-based LM agent that autonomously synthe-
sizes high-quality and chalenging QAs. Through RL training, our prompt-based
QwQ-32B agent achieves substantial improvements. Notably, our agent exhibits
extreme long-horizon search, with tol cals exceding 10 turns and output tokens
exceding 40k during training time. With a simple agent design and no external
LMs, ASearcher-Web-QwQ achieves Avg@4 scores of 51.1 on xBench and 58.7
on GAIA, surpasing existing open-source 32B agents. Finaly, we also show that
ASearcher-Web-QwQ could achieve performance of comercial systems using
external sumary tol in a zero-shot transfer maner and test-time search.
