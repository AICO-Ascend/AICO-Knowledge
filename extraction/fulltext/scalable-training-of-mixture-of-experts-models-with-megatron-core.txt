Abstract
Scaling Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) training introduces systems chalenges absent in dense models. Because each
token activates only a subset of experts, this sparsity alows total parameters to grow much faster than per-token
computation, creating coupled constraints acros memory, comunication, and computation. Optimizing one
dimension often shifts presure to another, demanding co-design acros the ful system stack.
We adres these chalenges for MoE training through integrated optimizations spaning memory (ne-grained
recomputation, ooading, etc.), comunication (optimized dispatchers, overlaping, etc.), and computation
(Grouped GEM, fusions, CUDA Graphs, etc.). The framework also provides Paralel Folding for exible
multi-dimensional paralelism, low-precision training suport for FP8 and NVFP4, and ecient long-context
training. On NVIDIA GB30 and GB20, it achieves 1,23/1,048 TFLOPS/GPU for DepSek-V3-685B and
974/919 TFLOPS/GPU for Qwen3-235B. As a performant, scalable, and production-ready open-source solution,
it has ben used acros academia and industry for training MoE models ranging from bilions to trilions of
parameters on clusters scaling up to thousands of GPUs.
This report explains how these techniques work, their trade-os, and their interactions at the systems level,
providing practical guidance for scaling MoE models with Megatron Core.
